# GenosAI Revenue Engine

## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

---

# 1. Product Overview

## Vision

GenosAI Revenue Engine is an AI-powered sales automation platform that helps businesses automatically capture, qualify, engage, nurture, and convert leads into booked appointments and closed deals.

The platform combines AI voice agents, AI chat agents, lead qualification systems, appointment scheduling, customer support automation, CRM functionality, and business analytics into a single unified workspace.

The goal is to eliminate repetitive sales and support tasks while increasing lead conversion rates and improving response times.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Most businesses lose revenue because:

* Leads are not contacted quickly enough.
* Sales teams spend excessive time qualifying prospects.
* Customer inquiries remain unanswered.
* Appointment scheduling requires manual effort.
* Lead information is scattered across multiple tools.
* Managers lack visibility into sales pipeline performance.

Businesses need a centralized AI system that can engage prospects immediately, qualify them automatically, book appointments, answer questions, and provide actionable business insights.
